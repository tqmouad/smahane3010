## How these numbers were made

- Tool: PyEphem 4.2.1 (a sky calculator), run once in the assistant's workspace. It is NOT part of the website.
- Place used: latitude 32.3373 N, longitude 6.3498 W, height 620 m. These are approximate values for Beni Mellal city, typed from memory, not looked up for the Krazza district. A few km of error changes the times by seconds only.
- Clock: Morocco was on UTC+0 with no summer time on that date (checked with the standard time zone database, `Africa/Casablanca`).
- Times are rounded to the minute. Real sunset can differ by a few minutes because of the mountains south of the city.

the Sun and seven planets (Mercury to Neptune) on their birth day (noon) with hers, took the
average distance in degrees, and turned it into a percentage: 100% − (average ÷ 180°) × 100.
